Accessibility statement

Last updated 4 November 2026

Our aim

We want everyone to be able to use this site. We build to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2, level AA.

What we have done

  • All pages work with a keyboard, with a visible focus outline on every control.
  • The animated entrance on the home page is decorative. It stops moving when your device is set to reduce motion, and the page’s content is available as ordinary text.
  • Text and controls meet colour-contrast requirements on the dark background.
  • Forms have labels and plain error messages.

Known limits

Our reels do not yet have captions or audio descriptions. We are working on adding them.

Tell us about a problem

If something on this site is hard to use, email pixelverse.va@gmail.com or call 85906 33822. Tell us the page and what happened, and we will help and fix it.
