Privacy policy

Last updated 9 November 2026

What this site collects

This site does not use cookies or analytics. If you use the contact form, the details you type (name, email, project type and message) are used only to reply to your enquiry.

How the contact form works

The form either opens your email app with your message ready to send, or sends it to the studio inbox through a form service. Either way, we read it at pixelverse.va@gmail.com and keep it only as long as needed to respond and to run the project.

Software purchases and licences

If you buy Volsplat, the purchase is handled by Dodo Payments, our merchant of record. They collect your payment details and tell us your name, email address, country, order details and licence key. We never see your card number. Dodo Payments’s privacy policy covers what they do with your data. When Volsplat starts, it sends your licence key, an anonymised identifier for your computer, and the app version to our licence service so we can confirm your plan and check for updates. We use this only to run licences and support.

Videos and fonts

Our reels are streamed from Wix’s media servers. When you play or preview a reel, your browser connects to those servers, which will receive your IP address and browser details as part of any web request. Fonts are served from this site.

Your choices

You can ask us to show, correct or delete anything you have sent us by emailing pixelverse.va@gmail.com.
