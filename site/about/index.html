About

Pixelverse is a creative studio in Chennai. We make images and films from nothing, and we help real places look like themselves.

Where visual storytelling meets creative technology.

We work in VFX, 3D and AI imagery: simulations, composites, modelled worlds and generative visuals, directed with the same care as a live shoot.

The same eye goes into spatial work. For brokers, developers and designers we shoot twilight exteriors, aerial views and cinematic walkthroughs, and we install landscapes that are low-maintenance and built around native plants and efficient irrigation.

Whatever the medium, the aim is the same: make something people haven’t seen before, and make it clear enough to understand at a glance.

What we make

How a project runs

  1. Brief

    We start with what the work needs to do and who needs to see it.

  2. Look development

    Frames, tests and references until the look is agreed.

  3. Production

    Shooting, simulating, modelling or generating, with progress shared along the way.

  4. Delivery

    Final files in the formats and sizes you need.
