Refund policy

Last updated 9 November 2026

14-day refunds

If Volsplat isn’t right for you, ask for a refund within 14 days of your first purchase of a plan and we will refund it in full. Email pixelverse.va@gmail.com with the email you bought with, or your order number.

Subscription renewals

You can cancel a subscription at any time and you won’t be charged again. We don’t refund the part of a period that has already started, except where the law requires it. If a renewal charge surprises you, write to us within 3 days of the charge and we will review it.

How refunds are paid

Refunds are issued by Dodo Payments, our merchant of record, to the payment method you used. Banks usually take 5 to 10 working days to show them. When a refund is issued, the licence key for that purchase stops working.

Your legal rights

This policy doesn’t limit any rights you have under consumer law where you live.
